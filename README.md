# 🏦 Bank Transaction Fraud Analysis using Power BI

## 📌 Project Overview

This project analyses bank transaction data to identify fraudulent activities using Microsoft Power BI. The objective was to transform raw transaction data into meaningful business insights through data cleaning, DAX measures, and interactive dashboards.

The analysis focuses on understanding fraud patterns, customer behaviour, transaction characteristics, and potential risk factors to support better fraud prevention strategies.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

- Analyse fraudulent and legitimate transactions.
- Calculate key fraud metrics and KPIs.
- Identify high-risk transaction channels.
- Understand customer and behavioural fraud patterns.
- Analyse transaction timing and location.
- Provide business recommendations to reduce fraud exposure.

---

# 🔍 Key Findings

- A total of 2,512 transactions were analysed.
- 126 transactions (5.02%) were identified as fraudulent.
- Fraudulent transactions had a significantly higher average value than legitimate transactions.
- Online banking accounted for the highest proportion of fraudulent transactions.
- Debit transactions represented the largest share of fraudulent transaction amounts.
- Most fraudulent transactions occurred after a single successful login attempt.
- Customers under the age of 25, particularly students, recorded the highest number of fraud cases.
- Fraud activity was concentrated during specific transaction times, days, and locations.
- Fraud resulted in approximately 79,791.45 in financial losses.

---

# 💼 Business Insights

The analysis revealed that although only 5.02% of transactions were fraudulent, they represented a significant financial risk due to their higher average transaction values. Online banking emerged as the highest-risk transaction channel, while debit transactions contributed the largest share of fraudulent transaction amounts. Customer demographics, login behaviour, transaction timing, and location also influenced fraud occurrence.

These findings demonstrate that effective fraud detection should consider multiple risk factors rather than relying on a single indicator.

---

# ✅ Business Recommendations

- Strengthen online banking security through Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA).
- Implement real-time monitoring for high-value transactions.
- Enhance fraud detection rules for debit transactions.
- Introduce risk-based authentication for suspicious logins, devices, and locations.
- Improve customer awareness through fraud prevention education.
- Strengthen identity verification procedures for in-branch transactions.
- Increase fraud monitoring during high-risk periods and in high-risk locations.
